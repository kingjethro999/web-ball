Local transformations
---------------------
The source mesh was assigned named football-kit material regions for runtime
club, goalkeeper, skin, hair and boot customization. Web Ball's pass, shot,
lob, tackle, slide, header, celebration, dejection, goalkeeper and referee
clips were authored locally on the same Quaternius skeleton with Blender.
The local transformations are part of Web Ball's source distribution.

Retrieved: 2026-09-28
Runtime export generated with Blender 5.2 LTS.
